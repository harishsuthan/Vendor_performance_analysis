# Vendor Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes vendor performance using sales, purchase, inventory, pricing, and profitability data. The objective is to identify opportunities to improve profitability, reduce inventory holding costs, optimize procurement, and minimize vendor-dependency risks.

## Business Objectives

- Identify low-performing brands that need promotional or pricing changes.
- Identify top vendors contributing to sales and gross profit.
- Analyze the cost benefits of bulk purchasing.
- Evaluate inventory turnover and unsold stock.
- Compare profitability between high- and low-performing vendors.

## Key Insights

- Identified **198 brands** with low sales but high profit margins, making them suitable for targeted promotions and pricing optimization.
- The **top 10 vendors** account for **65.69%** of total purchases, showing dependency on a small group of suppliers.
- Bulk purchasing resulted in approximately **72% lower unit costs**, supporting strategic high-volume procurement.
- Identified **$2.71M** in unsold inventory capital, indicating the need for better inventory planning.
- Low-performing vendors had a higher average profit margin (**41.55%**) than top vendors (**31.17%**), but lower sales volume.
- Statistical testing confirmed a significant difference in profit margins between top- and low-performing vendor groups.

## Recommendations

- Use targeted promotions for high-margin, low-sales brands.
- Diversify vendor partnerships to reduce supply-chain risk.
- Use bulk purchasing strategically to lower unit costs.
- Reduce slow-moving inventory through clearance sales and optimized purchase quantities.
- Improve pricing, marketing, and distribution for low-performing vendors.
